SECURITY, WITHOUT THE THEATER

Clear boundaries matter,
especially when the box is black.

Cobolt's core task runs on your Mac. “Local” is not just a marketing word: passwords, master keys, and decrypted bytes do not leave the native app through the web bridge. From Preferences, you can verify vault health, create an encrypted backup, and review temporary copies. We also say plainly that deleting from an SSD is not the same as forensic erasure.
