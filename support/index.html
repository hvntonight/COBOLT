FAQ / 02

Frequently asked

What if I forget my password?

If you enabled a recovery key, use it to restore access. Without both the password and recovery key, the vault cannot be recovered; Cobolt does not keep a backdoor key.

Are files really never uploaded?

The current core workflow runs locally on your Mac. File objects, catalogs, and unlock data are not uploaded to a Cobolt cloud. Apple StoreKit handles Cobolt Pro transactions.

Can Quick Look leave plaintext behind?

Some system preview flows may need a temporary decrypted copy. Cobolt works to clean it up on lock or exit; see the privacy policy for the full boundary.

What does Pro add?

The free plan includes one managed vault, up to 10 protected files, and 1GB per vault. Cobolt Pro removes those limits and enables user-selected locations and advanced storage features.
